How do I scan a receipt?

Tap New Receipt, then Scan. Take or choose a photo, crop it to the receipt, and tap Scan. In Settings › AI Model, choose Cloud or on-device reading with Apple Intelligence on supported devices.

How do credits work?

Cloud text scans use 1 credit; image scans use 2. Each new device starts with 20 free credits. Add 50, 150 or 450 in Settings › Credits. On-device scanning and entering items yourself are free.

How do I split and settle?

Add people, then tap an item to assign who shared it. Use the grid to see everyone at once. On Settle, choose who paid to see who pays them back. Group a trip’s receipts to settle them together.

Where are my receipts stored?

Your receipts stay on your device. There’s no account and no receipt syncing. Credit balances and purchases are kept on the server under a random ID.

Can I watch the welcome tour again?

Yes. Open Settings › Take the Tour whenever you want a refresher.
