Scanning a receipt

Tap New Receipt, then Scan, and take or choose a photo. Crop it so only the receipt shows, then tap Scan. Choose how receipts are read in Settings › AI Model: on this device with Apple Intelligence (on supported devices), or in the cloud.

Credits

  • Scanning on this device with Apple Intelligence, and typing items in, are always free.
  • Cloud scans use credits: 1 for a text scan, 2 for an image scan. Every new device starts with 20 free credits.
  • Buy more in Settings › Credits: 50, 150 or 450 credits. Credits never expire, and a failed scan gives its credits back.
  • Any purchase also lets you turn off the watermark on shared images.

Splitting and settling

  • Manage the people row in place: tap Edit or +, rename someone, or remove them.
  • Tap an item to choose who shared it, or tap a person's name to set everything they had.
  • Tap the grid button to see every item and person at once and tick who shared what. Each person's total is at the bottom.
  • Each item can be split equally, by percent, by amount or by quantity.
  • Service charge, VAT and discounts are added in order and shared in proportion.
  • Tap the share button to send the item list or the grid as an image.
  • On Settle, choose who paid to see who pays them back.
  • Put a trip's receipts in a group to settle them all with the fewest transfers. A group has a Receipts tab and a Summary tab.

Your data

Receipts are stored only on your device. There's no account and nothing is synced. Your credit balance and purchases are kept on SplitAss's server under a random ID, never with your receipts. To remove everything on your device, use Settings › Delete All Receipts, or delete the app. Read the Privacy Policy.
