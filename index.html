Scanning a receipt

Tap New Receipt, then Scan, and take or choose a photo. Crop it so only the receipt shows, then tap Scan. Choose how receipts are read in Settings › AI Model: on this device with Apple Intelligence (on supported devices), or by Gemini or OpenAI.

Splitting and settling

  • Tap an item to choose who shared it, or tap a person's name to set everything they had.
  • Each item can be split equally, by percent, by amount or by quantity.
  • Service charge, VAT and discounts are added in order and shared in proportion.
  • On Settle, choose who paid to see who pays them back.
  • Put a trip's receipts in a group to settle them all with the fewest transfers.

Your data

Receipts are stored only on your device. There's no account and nothing is synced. To remove everything, use Settings › Delete All Receipts, or delete the app. Read the Privacy Policy.
